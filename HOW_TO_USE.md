# THE BIG ASS CALENDAR — how to use it (the very simple version)

*Print me. Stick me near the desk. 2026-08-11.*

**Important:** today the calendar lives only inside Marco's MacBook. Part 1 puts it on the internet. Do Part 1 first — the phones come after.

---

## PART 1 — Marco's MacBook Pro · one time · ~15 minutes

1. Open **Terminal**.
2. Copy-paste this line and press Enter:
   `cd /Users/mcsk/_MCSK/_DOCs/_claude/_bigass_calendar && claude`
3. When Claude answers, say exactly:
   > **"Read CLAUDE.md. Do the one-time PRIVATE setup exactly as written there: install the requirements, generate the push keys, encrypt the calendar with a passphrase I will choose, and put it online on GitHub Pages. Ask me for the passphrase when you need it, and give me the final link."**
4. Follow anything Claude asks — it will ask you to **invent a passphrase**. Shape: **four or five random everyday words, small letters, with spaces** — like `walnut tango ladder moon` (don't use that one, invent your own; Claude can roll random words for you). No names, no birthdays, nothing from your crypto wallet. Write it on paper; you three will each type it once. It may also need your GitHub login once. At the end it gives you a **link**.
5. **WhatsApp that link to Inna.** Keep it yourself too. That link IS the calendar.

**How private is it?** What sits on the internet is scrambled nonsense. The passphrase unscrambles it — typed **once per device**, then that device stays unlocked.

## PART 2 — Marco's iPhone · 2 minutes · only after Part 1

1. Open the link in **Safari**. If the wall asks for the passphrase, type it (once).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** → **Add**.
4. Open the new **✳ icon** on your Home Screen.
5. Tap the **🔔** button → **Allow**.
6. Tap **"send to Marco 📤"** → choose **Notes** (a note to yourself).
7. Back on the MacBook: copy that note's text, open Claude in the calendar folder (step 1–2 above), and say:
   > **"File this subscription for Marco: (paste the text)"**

## PART 3 — Inna's iPhone · 2 minutes

1. She opens the link you sent — in **Safari** — and types the passphrase you gave her (once).
2. **Share** → **"Add to Home Screen"** → **Add**.
3. She opens the **✳ icon**, taps **🔔** → **Allow**.
4. She taps **"send to Marco 📤"** → WhatsApp → you.
5. You paste it to Claude: **"File this subscription for Inna: (paste)"**.

*If 🔔 shows instructions instead of asking permission — that's normal, it's telling her to do step 2 first. If it says the phone can't do it: Settings → General → Software Update (needs iOS 16.4 or newer).*

## PART 4 — Inna's MacBook Air · 30 seconds

1. Open the link in any browser. **Bookmark it.** That's all — it always shows the current wall.
2. Optional: tap 🔔 there too. If it works, pings on the Mac as well; if it complains, ignore it — the iPhone is the one that matters.

## If something looks wrong

- **Wall looks old on the phone** → tap **↻** (top of the screen).
- **No pings for days** → Marco says to Claude: *"test the push."*
- **Anything else** → Marco opens Claude in the folder and describes it in plain words. That's the repair shop.
