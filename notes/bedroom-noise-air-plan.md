# Bedroom: road noise + night air plan (researched 20 Oct 2026)

Room 3.5 × 6 × 2.5 m = 52.5 m³, 3 sleepers 21:00–07:30 (10.5 h). Road-facing wall = two double-glazed door-windows (~3.5 × 2.5 m total), exterior PVC roller shutter. Split AC recirculates only (no fresh air).

## Air (calculated; sleep CO2 ≈ 11 L/h per adult measured by DTU, child ~8 L/h assumed → ~30 L/h)
Fully sealed: >1000 ppm after ~1 h, ~6,450 ppm by 07:30. Leaky room (0.3 air changes/h): ~2,270 ppm. 0.5/h: ~1,590 ppm.
Fresh air needed: ~55 m³/h for ≤1000 ppm, ~32 m³/h for ≤1400 ppm. Evidence: sleep worsens above ~1000 ppm, aim ≤800–1000.
Moisture: ~1.1 kg water/night vs ~0.5 kg the room air holds → condensation on cold glass behind curtains in winter (mould).
Oxygen is not the issue (drops <1 point). Any gas appliance nearby → never seal, add EN 50291 CO alarm.

## Decisions
- Monitor: NDIR CO2 meter with audible alarm (SwitchBot Meter Pro CO2 ~€50, or Aranet4 ~€220 e-ink). Alarm at 1200 ppm. Watch RH ≤60%.
- Fresh air: from the QUIET side of the home (bedroom door open + window open in a room away from road), or a through-wall heat-recovery unit (Siegenia AEROPAC ~60 m³/h ~20 dB(A); Lunos e² max 38 m³/h).
- Noise: leaks first (window seals, shutter box "caixa de estore", strap hole), then heavy sealed layers. Curtains = 2–5 dB, ~0 on truck rumble. Real removable gain = rigid panels (board + Tecsound 5 kg/m²) gasketed into the frame.
- Earplugs: Leroy Merlin PT 3M orange foam 37 dB, 60-pack ~€11. Correct insertion matters most.
